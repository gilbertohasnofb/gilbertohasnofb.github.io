*The Wanderer’s Clarinet* is an algorithmic work for solo bass clarinet. Because of
that, it is recommended that the player generates a new version of this work for each
new performance (more on this below). Also, the player is welcome to play more than
one version in a single concert, in order to illustrate the algorithmic character of this
composition.
