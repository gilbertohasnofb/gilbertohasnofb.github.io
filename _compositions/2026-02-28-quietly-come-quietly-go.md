_The ocean is deathless_  
_The islands rise and die_  
_Quietly come, quietly go_  
_A silent swaying breath_

—Agnes Martin

_quietly come, quietly go_ is an algorithmic composition written for solo cimbalom. In this work, I continue exploring compositional ideas that have fascinated me over the last decade: how simple, strict rules and constraints, coupled with random selections, can be used to generate musical structure. Each individual rule governing this composition is relatively simple; however, when combined, they create complex and often unpredictable patterns of sound. The work also explores how simple linear processes (which are usually evident to the eye when looking at the score) can produce a listening experience that suggests a far richer and more complex design.

Throughout the piece, I make extensive use of musical loops, which repeat and emphasise these patterns of sound, generating a kaleidoscopic sense of constant yet slow movement; a cyclical approach to time, akin to being adrift. Underneath this movement lies a kind of stillness: change and repetition folding into each other until they become difficult to tell apart.

The piece closes with a quotation of Clarence Barlow's guitar composition _...until..., version 7_. Clarence was a dear friend and had an immense impact on my own trajectory as a composer, and this piece is written in his memory.
