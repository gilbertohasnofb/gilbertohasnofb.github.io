*Cycles* (2012) is an indeterminate composition for organ. The performer may choose their own path through the score, which is notated with musical fragments connected via arrows. The paths may reach the initial fragment an indeterminate amount of times, leading to as many cyclic waves of music as the performer wishes to play.
