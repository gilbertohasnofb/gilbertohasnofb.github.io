*Tête-à-Tête* is an algorithmic work for two marimbas. Because of that, it is recommended that the players generate a new version of
this work for each new performance (more on this below). Also, the players are welcome to play more than one version in a single
concert, in order to illustrate the algorithmic character of this composition.
