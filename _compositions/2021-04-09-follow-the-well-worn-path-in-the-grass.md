_follow the well-worn path in the grass_ (2021) is a computer-generated composition in which I continue to explore the themes of musical loops and perception of repetition, particularly within a soft and quiet aesthetics. This piece was composed using algorithmic methods and examines how simple linear processes, which are usually evident to the eye when looking at the score, can result in a listening experience that often suggests a far richer and more complex design.
