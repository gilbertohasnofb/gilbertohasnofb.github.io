*Don’t mind if I do!* is a generative work for two pianos. Because of this, it is recommended that the performers generate a new version
of this work for each new performance (more on this below). Also, the players are welcome to play more than one version in a single
concert, in order to illustrate the algorithmic character of this composition.

The players should hold the sustain pedal down throughout the whole work. If a player judges necessary to clear the pedal at some
point, it is recommended that he does so not very often and only on a loud note (red accented note). In the end of the piece, the
sustain pedal should remain held down for as long as necessary for the resonance to disappear.

There are no dynamic indications in this piece. The regular notes (black note heads and without accents) should be played as soft as
possible, while the red accented notes should be played as loud as possible.
