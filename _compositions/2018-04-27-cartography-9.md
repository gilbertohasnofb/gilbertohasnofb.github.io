*Cartography #9* is part of my series of algorithmic works entitled *Cartographies*. These are all ensemble works which explore how simple strict rules and constraints, coupled with random selections, can be used to generate musical structure. The rules governing each of these pieces are kept as simple as possible, each fitting on a single sheet of paper; but when applied together, these rules can often create complex patterns of sounds.
