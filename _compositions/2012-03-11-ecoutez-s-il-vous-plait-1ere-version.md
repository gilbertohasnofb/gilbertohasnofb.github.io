*Écoutez, s'il vous plaît! (1ère version)* was my first attempt at strict total serialism. In this composition, I attempted to not only control pitch and durations separately, but to ensure two more criteria: 1) that all transpositions of the prime and inverted dodecaphonic series appear exactly once, and 2) that every pitch-class would sound for the exact same amount of total time.
